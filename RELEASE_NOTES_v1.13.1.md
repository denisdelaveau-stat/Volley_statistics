# Release notes — v1.13.1

The action-entry court now uses two rows of three large pastel player circles on the orange surface. Shirt numbers and role labels use the matching dark role colour. The net has been removed; the dashed separator between front and back rows remains.

**⟳ Changer de joueur** is now outside the orange court, on the blue Capture surface immediately above **HISTORY**. Substitution filtering and logging are unchanged.

The release preserves the v1.13.0 Coach and Advanced Statistics features, Home buttons, unified JSON support and bundled reference fixture. The offline cache is `volley-live-capture-v1.13.1`.
